'> App\Models\Post::create(['

'. "title => "Finding Clarity in a World of Constant Pings",'

'. "author" => "Alex Mercer",'

5. Hit enter, now write another for column 'slug':
'. "slug" => "finding-clarity-in-a-world-of-constant-pings",'

6. Hit enter, write another for column 'body' (Note: due to its long paragraph, copy & paste the paragraph on it.  If there's a double quote in the value, use the backslash before the double quote \" to make it as string):
'. "body" => "We often mistake busyness for importance, filling every quiet moment with a scroll or a tap. But true productivity isn't about how quickly we respondit's about how intentionally we act.
